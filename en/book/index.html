Two sessions, no fitness religion

A simple entry point for healthy adults. Pain, injury or relevant limitations should be assessed individually first.

Session A — Gym

  1. Leg press — 2–3 sets
  2. Chest press — 2–3 sets
  3. Lat pulldown — 2–3 sets
  4. Seated row — 2 sets
  5. Lateral raise or shoulder machine — 2 sets

Choose a load that leaves a few clean reps in reserve. If you reach the top of your rep range with good technique, increase the load slightly next time.

Session B — Home

  1. Chair squat — 2–3 sets
  2. Elevated push-up — 2–3 sets
  3. Band row — 2–3 sets
  4. Hip hinge / Romanian deadlift with a backpack — 2 sets
  5. Lateral raise with light weights or band — 2 sets

The goal of the first eight weeks is consistency, not maximal exhaustion.
