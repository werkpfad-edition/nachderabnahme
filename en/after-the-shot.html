What the book is about

Many books explain how to lose weight with GLP-1-based medication. At goal weight, the guidance often becomes vague. AFTER THE SHOT starts precisely there. It treats the weight-loss phase as preparation for maintenance and combines the evidence with the practical problems that appear in ordinary life.

Understand

Why weight can return

Energy needs, hunger, satiety, food noise and the limits of simple “more discipline” explanations.

Prepare

Build the foundation early

Muscle, protein, strength training, tracking skills and a realistic goal weight.

Maintain

The first months at goal

Weight trends, weekends, restaurants, stress, emotional patterns and an escalation plan.
